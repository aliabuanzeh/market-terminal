# Market Terminal

Ali's personal live US market dashboard. Runs entirely in the browser — no server, no API keys, no AI tokens.

- Live ticker tape: indices, VIX, 10Y yield, dollar, oil, gold, bitcoin and your watchlist
- Clickable watchlist (add or remove any ticker, saved in your browser)
- Interactive TradingView chart — hover for the price at any point, switch timeframes, add indicators
- Technical gauge, company snapshot, financials and breaking news for the selected stock
- S&P 500 heatmap, indices / rates / sectors / macro overview
- Top market stories, US economic calendar, top gainers / losers / most active
- "Ask Claude" buttons that open a full research brief in Claude

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site.

Market data and news by [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/). Not investment advice.
